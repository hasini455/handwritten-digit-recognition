Building blocks

Convolution slides small filters over the image to detect strokes and edges. ReLU keeps positive activations. Pooling shrinks feature maps and adds shift tolerance. Flatten turns feature maps into a vector. Dense layers combine features. Dropout randomly disables units during training to reduce overfitting. Softmax converts 10 outputs into probabilities summing to 1.
