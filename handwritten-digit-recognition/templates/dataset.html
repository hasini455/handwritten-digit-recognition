MNIST

MNIST is a benchmark of handwritten digits loaded here through TensorFlow/Keras. It has 60,000 training and 10,000 test images, each 28 × 28 pixel grayscale, in 10 classes (0–9). The model learns from the training set; the separate test set measures how well it generalises to unseen images.
