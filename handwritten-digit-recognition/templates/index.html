How it works

Handwritten digit recognition maps a picture of a digit to one of ten classes. The image is cleaned and resized, a CNN extracts visual features (strokes, curves, loops), and a softmax layer outputs a probability for each digit.

Image Input→Preprocessing→CNN Model→Prediction→Evaluation

Why CNNs for vision?

Convolutional layers reuse small filters across the whole image, so they detect patterns wherever they appear while using far fewer parameters than fully connected networks. Pooling adds tolerance to small shifts, which suits handwriting.
